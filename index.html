Read. Track. Complete — together.

Nabd Al-Quran ("Pulse of the Quran") is an Arabic-first mushaf reader built around one powerful habit: completing the Quran, alone or together. Invite your family, friends, or community into a shared khatma — each reader receives a unique page, progress is visible across all 604 pages, and everyone shares the completion moment.

Offline mushaf reader

The complete Quran text (Hafs ʿan ʿĀsim, Uthmani script) bundled on your device. No internet needed to read — ever.

Personal khatma

Track your own reading across all 604 pages, keep your daily streak alive, and celebrate every completion.

Collective khatma

Start a group khatma with family or friends. Every reader takes a different page until the Quran is complete.

Dedication & sharing

Dedicate a khatma to a loved one or a cause, and share a beautiful completion card when it's done.

Ramadan mode

A 30-day juz-per-day rhythm for the blessed month, with gentle daily reminders.

Trust-first text

No AI generation, rewriting, or transformation is ever applied to Quranic text. What you read is the mushaf, exactly.

Our promise

  • No ads. There is no advertising anywhere in the app — and there never will be on Quran pages.
  • No third-party tracking. We do not sell data, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Offline first. Reading works completely without an internet connection.
