Privacy Policy

Nabd Al-Quran — نبض القرآن  |  Last updated: 2026-07-10  |  Contact: nalhamzy@gmail.com

Nabd Al-Quran is a Quran reading and khatma tracking app made by Ideal Intelligence. It is designed to be private by default: reading works fully offline, there are no ads, and there is no third-party advertising or cross-app tracking of any kind. This policy explains in plain language what data the app handles and how. If anything is unclear, email us and we will explain it.

1. The short version

  • No ads. The app contains no advertising and no advertising SDKs.
  • No tracking. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not use your data for third-party advertising. No advertising identifier (IDFA / AAID) is used.
  • No selling of data. We never sell, rent, or trade your data.
  • Offline first. The full Quran text is stored on your device. Reading and personal khatma tracking work entirely without an internet connection, and your reading progress is stored locally on your device.

2. What the app stores on your device

Your reading position, personal khatma progress, daily streak, and app settings (such as reminder time and theme) are stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you use the optional group features described below.

3. Optional collective khatma features

If you choose to join or create a shared (collective) khatma, the app uses Google Firebase to synchronize group progress. In that case the following limited data is processed:

Data Why Where
An anonymous account identifier To keep track of which pages you have claimed and completed in a shared khatma. No email, name, or phone number is required. Firebase Authentication (Google LLC)
Khatma participation data (pages claimed, pages completed, optional display name, optional dedication text you write) Core functionality of the shared khatma — so the group can see collective progress. Cloud Firestore (Google LLC)

If you never use the collective features, none of this data is created.

4. Diagnostics and analytics

The app may collect anonymous crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) and aggregate, non-identifying usage statistics (Firebase Analytics) — for example, how many devices opened the reader on a given day. This information is used solely to fix bugs and improve the app. It is not linked to your identity and is never used for advertising or shared with data brokers.

5. Sharing

When you share an invite link or a completion card, the app uses your device's native share sheet. Only the text or image you explicitly choose to share is sent, and only to the app you pick. We do not see or store what you share.

6. What we never collect

  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photos, or files.
  • No microphone or camera access.
  • No advertising identifiers.
  • No browsing history.

7. Children

The app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; the app requires no account, name, or email to use.

8. Data retention and deletion

Local data is deleted when you delete the app. If you used the collective khatma features and want the associated anonymous account data removed, email nalhamzy@gmail.com and we will delete it.

9. Service providers

We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Crashlytics, Analytics) as our infrastructure provider for the optional online features described above. Firebase processes this data on our behalf under Google's privacy and security terms.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be highlighted in the app's release notes.
