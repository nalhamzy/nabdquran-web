Frequently asked questions

Does the app work offline?

Yes. The complete Quran text is bundled with the app, and reading plus personal khatma tracking work entirely offline. An internet connection is only needed for the optional shared (collective) khatma features.

Is the Quran text authentic?

The app ships the Hafs ʿan ʿĀsim reading in Uthmani script from a verified open corpus. No AI generation, rewriting, or transformation is ever applied to Quranic text.

How does the group khatma work?

Create a khatma and share the invite link with family or friends. Each reader claims a unique page, so no two people read the same page, and the group's progress is visible across all 604 pages until the khatma is complete.

Are there ads?

No. There are no ads anywhere in the app, and there never will be on Quran pages. There is also no third-party tracking. See our Privacy Policy.

Do I need an account?

No account, email, or phone number is needed to read the Quran or track your personal khatma. Shared khatma features use an anonymous identifier only.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes all locally stored data. If you used the shared khatma features and want your anonymous account data removed from our servers, email us and we will delete it.
